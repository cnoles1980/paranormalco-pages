Privacy Policy

ParanormalCo. is designed to be playable without creating an account. This policy explains the information used by the iOS app and the choices available to players.

Game data

Your currencies, operation levels, investigators, event progress, settings, and other save data are stored locally on your device. ParanormalCo. does not currently provide an account system or cloud save service.

Advertising

ParanormalCo. includes support for Google AdMob. Advertising is centrally disabled for the initial release and may be enabled later for optional rewarded offers. Before requesting ads, the app uses Google’s consent tools where required. If advertising is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may process device information, IP address, advertising identifiers, consent choices, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to provide, measure, prevent fraud in, and improve advertising.

ParanormalCo. requests non-personalized ads by default. Optional rewarded ads are initiated by the player; declining one does not block required gameplay. Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy.

Tracking permission

If a future advertising configuration requires Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission, ParanormalCo. will ask before accessing data used to track activity across other companies’ apps or websites. Refusing that permission will not prevent normal play.

Purchases

If in-app purchases become available, Apple processes the transaction through the App Store. ParanormalCo. can read the resulting entitlement needed to unlock the purchased feature but does not receive your payment-card details.

Diagnostics and Apple services

Apple may provide aggregated App Store, sales, usage, and crash information under Apple’s own terms and your device privacy settings. ParanormalCo. does not currently operate a custom analytics account or player-profile database.

Deleting your data

Because the current save is local and there is no ParanormalCo. account, removing the app removes the app’s locally stored save data from that device. Apple device backups may be governed by your own iCloud or device-backup settings.

Changes

This policy will be updated before materially different data practices are enabled. The date at the top identifies the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact method on the ParanormalCo. support page. Do not include passwords, payment information, government identifiers, or other sensitive personal information.
